Node properties are the following:
Pokemon {id: INTEGER, name: STRING, generation: INTEGER},PokeType {name: STRING},Ability {name: STRING},Move {id: INTEGER, name: STRING, power: INTEGER, damage_class: STRING},MoveMethod {name: STRING},Learning {level: INTEGER}
Relationship properties are the following:
HAS_TYPE {slot: INTEGER},HAS_ABILITY {is_hidden: BOOLEAN, slot: INTEGER},EFFECTIVE_AGAINST {factor: INTEGER}
The relationships are the following:
(:Pokemon)-[:HAS_TYPE]->(:PokeType)
(:Pokemon)-[:HAS_ABILITY]->(:Ability)
(:Move)-[:OF_TYPE]->(:PokeType)
(:Pokemon)-[:LEARNS]->(:Learning)
(:Learning)-[:OF_MOVE]->(:Move)
(:Learning)-[:VIA]->(:MoveMethod)
(:PokeType)-[:EFFECTIVE_AGAINST]->(:PokeType)
A Learning node reifies one way a Pokemon can learn a Move: it links the Pokemon, the Move, and the MoveMethod, and carries the level at which it is learned.
