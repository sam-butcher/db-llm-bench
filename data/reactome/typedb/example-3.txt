Question: How many human complexes (species includes Homo sapiens) that have at least one direct component have every one of their direct components be an EntityWithAccessionedSequence?
```
match
  $c isa complex;
  species-assignment (classified-thing: $c, species: $sp);
  $sp has display-name "Homo sapiens";
  complex-composition (containing-complex: $c, component: $any);
  not {
    complex-composition (containing-complex: $c, component: $bad);
    not { $bad isa entity-with-accessioned-sequence; };
  };
select $c;
distinct;
reduce $count = count;
```
