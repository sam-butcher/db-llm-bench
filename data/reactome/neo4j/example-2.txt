Question: Across human reaction-like events (species includes Homo sapiens), how many distinct pairings of an event with one of its compartments are recorded? Count each event-and-compartment pairing separately, so an event assigned three compartments contributes three pairings.
```
MATCH (r:ReactionLikeEvent)-[:species]->(:Species {displayName:'Homo sapiens'})
MATCH (r)-[:compartment]->(c)
RETURN count(DISTINCT [elementId(r), elementId(c)])
```
